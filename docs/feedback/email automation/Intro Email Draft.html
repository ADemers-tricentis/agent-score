Thanks for your interest in AgentScore. Teams are shipping AI agents faster than they can tell whether those agents work, and AgentScore is Tricentis's answer. Point it at an agent's OpenTelemetry trace and you get a 0-100 score, a ship / warn / block verdict, and the exact span where things went wrong, with a suggested fix. It works with any agent and any vendor, and there's no proprietary SDK to install.
