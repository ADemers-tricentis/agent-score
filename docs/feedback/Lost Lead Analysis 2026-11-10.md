**What this does and does not show.** Reply rates look the same for v1 and v2 (about 12% on comparable age), which matches the tracker's "no difference", but the data cannot test it. The wording barely changed, send dates and format moved together (no overlap), and no cohort was randomized. Send date, company type and age explain more than format does: the Aug 20 bulk batch of 32 got 0 replies within 7 days, while the cohorts sent on the day of signup (Sep 17: 2 of 19, Oct 22: 1 of 22, Oct 28: 2 of 6) each got replies within a week. The form's relationship field also differs a little (Partner 4 of 24, Customer 3 of 29) but the groups are too small to read. Follow-up v2 cannot be judged yet. Verdict: cannot tell. A real test needs two formats sent to randomized halves of the same batch.
