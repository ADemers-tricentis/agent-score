## Decisions Made
- Reuse the engineer's existing audited OSS/license list for Accenture legal rather than producing a new one.
- Preferred kill-switch approach: **call-home license server**, with an **encrypted expiring license file** as fallback.
- Deliver on-prem as a docker-compose / Kubernetes deployment package (updatable components, persistent data).
- Reject embedding the kill-switch inside the container (insecure).
- Trial credits will be **granted manually** by the PM via the licensing service (no auto/unlimited credits).
- EvalClaw team onboards via a custom profile the engineer creates from their dimensions.
