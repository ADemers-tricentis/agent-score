## So what for AgentScore
Two head-to-head competitors raised prices meaningfully this quarter - LangSmith roughly doubled trace pricing and W&B Weave's Evals tier jumped 140% - while AgentScore still has no public pricing (a gap the competitive-analysis-report already flags as blocking external conversion). That's a live opening to lead with cost once pricing is public, not just features. Separately, Maxim AI's new retroactive-eval-on-historical-logs feature is the closest any competitor has come to AgentScore's own "grade what already happened, zero setup" wedge - it still requires Maxim's own SDK instrumentation rather than auto-profiling raw OTel traces, so the differentiation holds, but it's worth tracking as the gap narrows.
