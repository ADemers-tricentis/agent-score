## Langfuse
- **Update:** August 2026 shipped a new evals setup flow, multi-modal evaluators, and a CLI 1.0 release, on top of the Aug 17 "Langfuse v4" data-model rebuild (faster tables/dashboards at scale; Langfuse Cloud deprecates v3 endpoints Nov 16, 2026). ([Langfuse blog](https://langfuse.com/blog/2026-08-31-langfuse-august-update), [changelog](https://langfuse.com/changelog/2026-08-17-langfuse-v4))

([Gartner Reviews](https://www.gartner.com/reviews/product/langfuse-658437652))

## Braintrust
- **Update:** Cut its top self-serve "Pro" plan price ~75%, from $1K/mo to $249/mo (Aug 11, 2026), while keeping the same token/data/score usage-meter structure. ([Costbench](https://costbench.com/changelog/braintrust-price-decrease-2026-08/))
- **Customers love:** A 5.0-rating G2 reviewer called it "really powerful for building AI apps" - an all-in-one combo of evals, observability, and a fast playground for rapid experimentation; the same review had flagged the lack of self-serve pricing, which the August cut now addresses. ([G2](https://www.g2.com/products/braintrust-2024-12-22/reviews))
- **Using it for:** Temporal's integration guide shows teams pairing Braintrust with Temporal's durable-execution workflows for end-to-end visibility into model calls, retries, tool usage, and prompt versions when debugging agentic workflows. ([Temporal](https://temporal.io/changelog/braintrust-integration))

## Arize AI
- **Update:** Arize AX's August 2026 release added a full expression language for the sessions table, new REST endpoints for dataset splits/experiment tags, and OAuth2 workload-identity improvements. ([Arize docs](https://arize.com/docs/ax/release-notes/history/2026/08-2026))
- **Customers love:** Holds a 4.2/5 on G2 (28 reviews); reviewers consistently cite intuitive navigation, strong ML-workflow integrations, and detailed model-performance analysis, plus a 9.4 support-responsiveness score. ([G2](https://g2.com/products/arize-ai/reviews))
- **Using it for:** LG Uplus built an evaluation-driven development workflow on Arize AX for its AI Contact Center (30M subscribers) - combining production traces, user feedback, and domain expertise into eval datasets targeting tool-selection/routing quality, lifting agent accuracy from 70% to 96%. ([Arize](https://arize.com/customers/how-lg-uplus-is-building-better-agents-with-evaluation-driven-development-and-arize-ax/))

## So what for AgentScore
LangSmith just shipped Okta-based SSO/SAML + SCIM - exactly the enterprise-auth gap our own report flags as the #1 blocker ("table stakes we cannot sell without") - which narrows our list of competitors still missing it and raises the pressure to land RBAC/SSO once Tosca SSO is in place. Separately, two smaller signals reinforce gaps we already know about rather than opening new ones: a Maxim customer explicitly switched away from Braintrust for simulation capability Braintrust lacks (validating Gap #4, simulation, as a real switching factor, not just a theoretical one), and DeepEval's new MCP server eval support is a reminder that the window to be first to ship "MCP-aware" OTel trace labeling (our own low-effort roadmap idea) is closing.
