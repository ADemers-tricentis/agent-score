## So what for AgentScore
Two threads worth tracking together. Arize's Alyx 2.0 pushes further into "diagnose and act" territory - an autonomous planning agent spanning the AI engineering lifecycle - which narrows the gap on AgentScore's own differentiated ingest→score→attribution→recommendation pipeline faster than attribution alone would have. Separately, DeepEval's new calibrated-probability judge and Opik's similar Jev-based classifier both converge on cheap, low-latency judges as an alternative to frontier LLM-as-judge - exactly the cost/latency regime (Capability Gap #5) AgentScore's current Bedrock-judge stack doesn't address, and now two more competitors besides Galileo are moving there.
