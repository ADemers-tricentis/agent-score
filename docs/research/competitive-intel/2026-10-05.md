## So what for AgentScore
Dynatrace closed the Arize deal on Oct 1, and Alyx 2.0 pushes Arize from scoring traces toward an agent that plans the engineering work. LangSmith Engine v2 red-teams agents before production, which is an observability vendor stepping into the pre-ship testing gap, and LangSmith also shipped Okta SSO and SCIM. DeepEval's Jev judge and Opik's SQL and classifier checks are cheaper alternatives to a frontier LLM-as-judge. Maxim's voice simulation, and a reviewer who left Braintrust for Maxim's simulation, widen that same pre-ship gap into phone calls. Braintrust and LangSmith both cut seat prices in August. LangSmith's July per-trace increase is still in place.
