For the PM and designer. "Did the agent do the right thing?" is the existing Correct / Incorrect choice in the review queue. "Whose fault" is failure triage (workflow step 5): an LLM guesses, the expert confirms. The workflow only asks the expert to check 20% of guesses, and they should agree at least 90% of the time. "What kind?" is the error category. The LLM guesses it from the judge's reason, at the same time as the fault, and a run can have more than one kind. Each fault has its own short list, and the customer can add kinds their team uses. The first 10 labels are blind: scores and AI guesses show only after the expert answers, so those labels can later be used to check how well the automatic checks agree with the expert (workflow step 10). Several kinds already line up with a check: "Repeated the same step" with no_retry_loop, "Changed something it shouldn't" with the forbidden actions, "Didn't ask when it should have" with escalate-once, and the system kinds with invalid_if.
